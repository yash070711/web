MGU Assignment

Assign products to MGUs that will configure and distribute them.

Products you create stay owned by your risk carrier. Toggle an MGU to assign a product to it — the MGU will see the product in its Assigned Products catalogue and can configure Question, Risk, Eligibility, Underwriting and Rating studios.
